## L-2026-10-17-07 — The raw bias is class-dependent for sTDA-xTB (0 vs +0.75–1.0 eV), less so for the KS gap

**Method.** Acenes in the shard vs experimental lowest bright/lowest singlet energies compiled by Malloci et al.,
*Chem. Phys.* 2007 (10.1016/j.chemphys.2007.07.046), Tables 2–5 (range over gas, Ne matrix, n-heptane; midpoint
used). Compared with -06. → `outputs/extract/acene-exp-calibration.txt`.
**Result.** Offset vs experiment (observable − exp midpoint) [eV]: sTDA S1 naphthalene +0.156, anthracene −0.124,
tetracene −0.052, pentacene +0.013 (mean −0.002, SD 0.119). PubChemQC KS gap +0.924 (naphthalene, whose S1 is the
dark Lb state; vs its bright La 4.45 eV the offset is +0.46), +0.349, +0.271, +0.182. HOPV OPV molecules: S1 +0.75
(DFT geom) / +1.00 (UFF), KS +0.29.
**Interpretation.** sTDA-xTB is nearly unbiased for acenes and ~1 eV high for donor–acceptor OPV molecules, so no
single correction or window transfers between the two classes; the KS gap's offset stays within +0.18 to +0.46 eV
for bright states in both classes. A generic database mixes classes, so any one-window screen on an
uncalibrated cheap observable carries a class-dependent error of the order of the window width (1.1 eV).
**Verified against.** Malloci et al. 2007 Tables 2–5 (primary paper) + `stda_all.csv`; challenged for basis/geometry confounding (-15).
**Caveats.** Acenes n = 4, exp values span 0.1–0.2 eV by medium; two different KS levels (6-31G* PubChemQC vs
def2-SVP HOPV); class assignment is by dataset, not by a descriptor.
**Claim link.** C-central (revised) · C-calib.

**Result.** Pentacene B3LYP gap [eV]: def2-SVP/UFF 1.884 · 6-31G*/UFF 1.896 · 6-31G*/B3LYP-opt 2.209 · PubChemQC
2.397 · exp La 2.12–2.31. KS − exp (bright La midpoint), def2-SVP/UFF: naphthalene +0.087, anthracene −0.113,
tetracene −0.229, pentacene −0.331 (mean −0.147, SD 0.179); PubChemQC 6-31G*: +0.459, +0.349, +0.271, +0.182.
**Interpretation.** At fixed geometry the basis set moves the gap by 0.01 eV, so the def2-SVP (HOPV) vs 6-31G*

bias stands, with its magnitude reduced from ~0.9 to ~0.5 eV.

(`-06`: +0.245 ± 0.183), not computed on acenes; PubChemQC geometry not on disk.
